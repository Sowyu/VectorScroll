Download VectorScroll 1.7.0

Mac

Universal .dmg

Intel and Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later.

Install

  1. Open the DMG and drag VectorScroll into Applications.
  2. Open VectorScroll. A two page setup guide asks for Input Monitoring and Accessibility, one at a time, and moves on by itself as soon as macOS registers each grant.
  3. Press the middle button on any page.

That is the only manual install. Every later version installs itself from inside the app. See updates.
