Free

A better scroll should not cost anything. No licence, no account, no upsell.

Open source

MIT licensed Swift. Read it, fork it, or fix the one thing that bugs you.

Small

Swift and AppKit only. One 830 KB binary for Intel and Apple Silicon. No Electron.

Careful

A middle click stays a middle click. Nothing scrolls until the pointer leaves a 10 pt dead zone.
